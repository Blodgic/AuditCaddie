## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the acceptable use of [COMPANY NAME]'s information systems, network, devices, and data. It protects [COMPANY NAME], its employees, and its customers from security incidents, legal liability, and reputational harm caused by misuse.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and vendors who use [COMPANY NAME] systems, devices, or network access — regardless of location.

## 3. General Principles

[COMPANY NAME] provides technology resources to support legitimate business activities. All users are expected to:

- Use resources responsibly and professionally
- Protect confidential information at all times
- Report suspected misuse or security incidents promptly
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies

## 4. Acceptable Use

The following activities are permitted:

- Work-related communication via company email, Slack, and approved tools
- Access to company systems required for job duties
- Reasonable personal use that does not interfere with work performance or consume significant resources
- Use of approved software and cloud services for business purposes

## 5. Prohibited Activities

The following are strictly prohibited:

### 5.1 Security and Unauthorized Access
- Accessing systems, data, or accounts you are not authorized to access
- Attempting to bypass security controls (firewalls, MFA, access controls)
- Installing unauthorized software, VPNs, or remote access tools
- Port scanning, vulnerability scanning, or penetration testing on any network without explicit written authorization
- Sharing credentials or allowing others to use your accounts

### 5.2 Data and Privacy
- Downloading or transmitting customer data to unauthorized locations (personal devices, personal cloud storage)
- Sharing Confidential information externally without legal approval
- Accessing, modifying, or deleting data you are not authorized to handle
- Using company data for personal benefit

### 5.3 Malicious Activity
- Distributing malware, ransomware, or other malicious code
- Launching denial-of-service attacks
- Phishing, social engineering, or impersonation
- Using company resources for cryptocurrency mining

### 5.4 Legal and Compliance
- Using company resources to violate any applicable law or regulation
- Infringing on intellectual property rights (software piracy, copyright violations)
- Harassment, discrimination, or hostile communication via company systems
- Engaging in activities that create legal liability for [COMPANY NAME]

### 5.5 Resource Misuse
- Using company systems for personal commercial activities
- Excessive personal use that impacts performance or availability
- Storing large volumes of personal files on company systems
- Circumventing IT asset management or MDM enrollment

## 6. Device Use

### 6.1 Company-Issued Devices
Company-issued devices are provided for work purposes. Users must:
- Keep devices physically secure at all times
- Never leave devices unattended in public without locking the screen
- Immediately report lost or stolen devices to IT Security
- Not disable or circumvent encryption, MDM, or security software

### 6.2 Personal Devices (BYOD)
Personal devices may only be used for company work if:
- Approved by IT Security in writing
- Enrolled in company MDM (if required)
- Company data is accessed via approved, managed applications only

## 7. Email and Communication

- Company email is for business use; excessive personal use is not permitted
- Do not use personal email accounts for company business
- Do not open unexpected attachments or click links from unknown senders
- Report suspected phishing emails to [SECURITY EMAIL] immediately — do not forward to others
- Email is not considered secure for transmitting Confidential data — use encrypted channels

## 8. Internet and Cloud Services

- Company internet access may be monitored for security purposes
- Use of personal cloud storage (Google Drive personal, Dropbox, iCloud) for company data is prohibited
- All SaaS tools used for company work must be approved by IT Security
- Requests for new SaaS tools are submitted via [TICKETING SYSTEM]

## 9. Monitoring

[COMPANY NAME] reserves the right to monitor activity on its systems, network, and devices for security and compliance purposes. Users have no expectation of privacy on company-owned systems or networks. Monitoring activities comply with applicable laws.

## 10. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Revocation of system access
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Legal action where criminal conduct is involved
- Personal liability for damages caused

## 11. Reporting Violations

Report suspected violations to [SECURITY EMAIL] or directly to [MANAGER / HR]. Reports may be made anonymously via [REPORTING CHANNEL].
