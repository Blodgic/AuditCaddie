## 1. Purpose

[COMPANY NAME] is committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity and ethical behavior in everything we do. This Code of Conduct defines the standards of conduct expected of all employees, contractors, and representatives of [COMPANY NAME], and forms the foundation of our internal control environment as required by SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria CC1.1 and CC1.2.

## 2. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All full-time and part-time employees
- Contractors and consultants
- Third-party service providers acting on behalf of [COMPANY NAME]
- Board members and advisors

## 3. Core Principles

### 3.1 Integrity and Honesty

All personnel must act with honesty and transparency in all business dealings. Employees must not make false statements, misrepresent facts, or engage in any form of deception — whether with customers, partners, regulators, or colleagues.

### 3.2 Ethical Behavior

Personnel are expected to:
- Act in the best interests of [COMPANY NAME] and its customers
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any potential conflicts to their manager and [LEGAL / COMPLIANCE CONTACT]
- Refuse to offer, give, solicit, or accept bribes or improper payments
- Protect confidential and proprietary information

### 3.3 Respect in the Workplace

[COMPANY NAME] is committed to providing a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. All personnel must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with dignity and respect regardless of race, gender, age, religion, national origin, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic.

### 3.4 Compliance with Laws and Regulations

All personnel must comply with applicable laws, regulations, and contractual obligations. This includes data privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), export controls, anti-corruption laws, and securities regulations.

## 4. Information Security Responsibilities

### 4.1 Protecting Company and Customer Data

All personnel must:
- Handle confidential and customer data in accordance with [COMPANY NAME]'s Data Classification Policy
- Use only approved systems and tools for storing or processing company data
- Report any suspected data breaches or security incidents immediately to [SECURITY CONTACT / security@[company].com]
- Not share credentials, access keys, or passwords with others

### 4.2 Acceptable Use of Company Resources

Company systems, networks, and devices are to be used primarily for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not:
- Violate any provision of this Code of Conduct
- Expose company systems to security risks
- Consume excessive resources

### 4.3 No Unauthorized Access

Personnel must not attempt to access systems, data, or facilities beyond their authorized scope. This includes accessing customer data without a legitimate business need.

## 5. Conflict of Interest

Personnel must disclose any actual or perceived conflicts of interest to their manager and [LEGAL / HR CONTACT] before engaging in activities that could conflict with their responsibilities to [COMPANY NAME]. Examples include:
- Outside employment or consulting with competitors or customers
- Personal financial interests in vendors or partners
- Family or close personal relationships with vendors, customers, or competitors

## 6. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure

All personnel are required to protect confidential business information, including:
- Customer data and personally identifiable information (PII)
- Proprietary technology and source code
- Business strategies, financials, and contracts
- Any information marked as "Confidential" or "Restricted" per [COMPANY NAME]'s Data Classification Policy

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of employment or engagement.

## 7. Reporting Violations

### 7.1 How to Report

Personnel who observe, suspect, or are aware of violations of this Code of Conduct must report them promptly to:
- Their direct manager
- [HR CONTACT] at [hr@[company].com]
- [LEGAL / COMPLIANCE CONTACT]
- Anonymously via [ANONYMOUS REPORTING CHANNEL, if applicable]

### 7.2 Non-Retaliation

[COMPANY NAME] prohibits retaliation against any person who reports a potential violation in good faith. Retaliation itself is a violation of this Code of Conduct and will result in disciplinary action.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct will be investigated and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or engagement. Certain violations may also result in civil or criminal liability.

## 9. Acknowledgment

All personnel are required to read, understand, and sign an acknowledgment of this Code of Conduct upon hire and annually thereafter. Signed acknowledgments are retained by [HR / People Operations] as evidence of compliance.
