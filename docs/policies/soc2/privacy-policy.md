## 1. Introduction

[COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our customers and their end users. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information, and the rights available to individuals whose data we process.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account and Contact Data

| Data Category | Examples | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---------------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Identity | Name, job title, company name | Account creation, service delivery | Contract |
| Contact | Email address, phone number | Communications, support | Contract |
| Authentication | Username, hashed password, MFA tokens | Account security | Contract |
| Billing | Billing address, payment method (tokenized) | Payment processing | Contract |

### 2.2 Usage and Technical Data

| Data Category | Examples | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---------------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Service usage | Features used, frequency, session duration | Service improvement, support | Legitimate interest |
| Technical | IP address, browser type, device type | Security monitoring, fraud prevention | Legitimate interest |
| Audit logs | Actions taken within the platform | Security, compliance | Legal obligation |

### 2.3 Customer-Uploaded Data

[COMPANY NAME] processes data that customers upload to our platform. This data is:
- Processed only on behalf of and under the instructions of the customer (data controller)
- Not used for any secondary purpose
- Governed by our Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- Not shared with any third party without the customer's authorization

## 3. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to:
- Provide, operate, and improve our services
- Process payments and manage customer accounts
- Provide customer support
- Send service-related communications (invoices, security alerts, product updates)
- Monitor and ensure the security of our platform
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not**:
- Sell personal information to third parties
- Use customer data for advertising
- Use customer-uploaded data to train AI models without explicit consent

## 4. Privacy Choices and Consent

### 4.1 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of marketing emails at any time by:
- Clicking the unsubscribe link in any marketing email
- Emailing [privacy@[company].com] with an opt-out request

### 4.2 Cookies

We use cookies for:
- **Essential cookies:** Required for the service to function (session management)
- **Analytics cookies:** Usage analytics to improve the service

Non-essential cookies can be rejected via our [cookie preference center / browser settings].

### 4.3 Data Portability

You can request an export of your data by emailing [privacy@[company].com].

## 5. Data Subject Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

| Right | Description | How to Exercise |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Access** | Request a copy of your personal data | Email [privacy@[company].com] |
| **Correction** | Correct inaccurate or incomplete data | Via account settings or email |
| **Deletion** | Request deletion of your personal data | Email [privacy@[company].com] |
| **Restriction** | Restrict processing in certain circumstances | Email [privacy@[company].com] |
| **Portability** | Receive data in a machine-readable format | Email [privacy@[company].com] |
| **Objection** | Object to processing based on legitimate interest | Email [privacy@[company].com] |

We respond to all rights requests within **30 days**. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

## 6. Data Sharing and Sub-Processors

We share personal data with the following categories of third parties:
- **Cloud infrastructure providers** (e.g., AWS) — to operate our service
- **Payment processors** (e.g., [Stripe]) — to process payments
- **Customer support tools** (e.g., [Intercom / Zendesk]) — to provide support
- **Analytics tools** (e.g., [Mixpanel / Amplitude]) — to understand service usage

A complete list of sub-processors is available at [https://[company].com/sub-processors].

We execute Data Processing Agreements with all sub-processors that handle personal data.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill legal obligations. See our [Data Retention & Disposal Policy] for specific retention periods.

Upon account termination, we delete or anonymize personal data within **[30 days]** unless legal retention requirements apply.

## 8. Data Security

We protect personal data using:
- Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Multi-factor authentication for system access
- Regular security assessments and penetration testing
- Employee security training

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected individuals and regulators as required by applicable law.

## 9. International Data Transfers

[COMPANY NAME] is based in [USA]. If we transfer personal data from the [EU / UK / other] to the USA, we use appropriate transfer mechanisms:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU/EEA data
- [UK IDTA / Adequacy decision / other] for UK data

## 10. Privacy Inquiries and Complaints

For privacy questions, concerns, or complaints:

**Privacy Contact:** [privacy@[company].com]

We will acknowledge your inquiry within [5 business days] and resolve it within [30 days].

If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
